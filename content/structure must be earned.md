Structure must be earned (instead of using someone else's)

十分感同身受。我认为各种 [[obsidian|Obsidian]] 的开箱即用库或者 TiddlyWiki Projectify 等插件都不能让人真的更好地记录笔记（没有说这些开箱即用库和插件不好的意思，相反这对于社区很有益处）。在我看来，Starting from scratch 更能建立起一个完全适配自己的[[my note taking rules|笔记系统]]。

The 4 Rules of ideas:

- The worst way to learn is to connect the new to the nothing.
- The best way to learn is to connect the new to the known.
- The best way to remember is to reconnect the known to the known.
- The best way to imagine is to connect the known to the unknown.
